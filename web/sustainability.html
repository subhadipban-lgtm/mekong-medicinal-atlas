Sustainability, climate risk and cultivation

Screening layers built from the hydroclimatic synthesis of Tiwari et al. 2023 (Scientific Data, Zenodo 7803254, CC-BY-4.0). These are forward-looking screening tools, not conservation or agronomic recommendations: the harvest-pressure index is a proxy, not measured offtake; future scenarios are conditional projections, not forecasts; cultivation suitability needs field validation.

Methods and reproducibility

  • Harmonization — 5 km Albers equal-area grid, 31,922 in-basin cells, per-variable resampling rules.
  • Harvest pressure — SSP2-2050 population × species richness × evidence-weighted use score.
  • Hydrological fragmentation — distance to 232 major dams, 10 km threshold.
  • SDM and cultivation methods will be published with their results; the pipeline is scripts/sustainability/ and the driver is scripts/sustainability/run_sustainability.sh.
