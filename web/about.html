Scope

The atlas covers medicinal vascular plants recorded in the Mekong basin or reported for it from Cambodia, China (Yunnan and Guangxi), Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam. One entry is an alga and is flagged as such. Occurrence records are not evidence of medicinal use.
