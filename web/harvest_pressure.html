Harvest pressure & hydrological risk

Two operational indices of conservation pressure on the atlas taxa: a harvest-pressure proxy (2050 population density × species richness × graded local use score, quantile-tiered) and a hydrological-fragmentation index (share of each taxon's in-basin occurrences within 10 km of a major dam). Methods: harvest pressure · hydrological fragmentation.
