Evidence engine — pilot, 20 taxa

Historical pilot search: 20 taxa with the most compiled pharmacology records (Milestone 1 of the evidence engine, generated 2026-11-26). 2,317 provisionally classified retrieval records representing 1,880 unique publications, deduplicated by DOI, then PMID, then normalized title; 729 rows flagged review and await a human queue. Candidate publications were retrieved from Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref and DOAJ and classified by an automated rubric; classification is machine-made and never presented as evidence strength.

These are retrieval candidates. Species-specific claim support has not been assessed. The 50-paper stratified audit is incomplete. The historical full-corpus file was also affected by a pagination defect; its size does not establish completeness. Expanded all-atlas retrieval is being reconciled separately.

Expanded historical retrieval: 17,787 candidate species–publication rows across 20 taxa, representing 15,172 distinct publication keys. 9,605 rows require classification review. This corpus contains the pilot slice and must not be added to it. It remains incomplete and is not verified species-specific evidence.

GMS deep-search register (Semantic Scholar retrieval and screening)

A separate register (GMS_DEEP_SEARCH, disjoint from the pilot corpus by register discipline) holding a separate screening pipeline over Semantic Scholar: 10 included papers, 175 plants extracted (168 matched in WCVP), 190 plant–molecule rows. Molecule/activity content is LLM-extracted from abstracts and treated as leads, not curated facts. The register tables are available from the authors on request.
