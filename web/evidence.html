Evidence engine — pilot, 20 taxa

Pilot set: 20 taxa with the most pharmacology records (Milestone 1 of the evidence engine, generated 2026-11-25). Publications were retrieved from Europe PMC and OpenAlex and classified by an automated rubric; rows the rubric cannot place are flagged review and await a human queue. Classification is machine-made and never presented as evidence strength.

GMS deep-search register (Semantic Scholar systematic review)

A separate register (GMS_DEEP_SEARCH, disjoint from the pilot corpus by register discipline) holding another session's PRISMA pipeline over Semantic Scholar: 10 included papers, 175 plants extracted (168 matched in WCVP), 190 plant–molecule rows. Molecule/activity content is LLM-extracted from abstracts and treated as leads, not curated facts. The register tables are available from the authors on request.
