Licence

The occurrence archive is released under CC-BY-NC-4.0 because it includes records published under that licence; each record's own licence (CC0, CC-BY-4.0 or CC-BY-NC-4.0) is given in its license field and prevails for that record. The basin boundary is CC-BY 4.0 (Tiwari et al.). Natural Earth layers are public domain. The project tables are a research compilation whose rows cite their primary sources; the source workbook is not redistributed.
